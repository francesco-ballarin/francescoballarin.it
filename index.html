Highlights

New preprint on proving the inf-sup condition for the semidiscrete HiMod reduction of Stokes problems, by E. Temellini, F. Ballarin, T. Chacón Rebollo, S. Perotto (August 2025).

New preprint on global-in-time iterative decoupled POD-Galerkin reduced order models for linear poroelasticity, by H. Gu, F. Ballarin, M. Cai, J. Li (August 2025).

I joined the Debian Project as Debian Developer (July 2025).

I was appointed director of MATHEX, the research center of Università Cattolica on applications of mathematics and computer science (May 2025).
